## 👋 About me

I'm a robotics engineer with **7+ years of hands-on experience**, working across electronics, embedded software, mechanical integration, and autonomous systems. I enjoy taking an idea through design, prototyping, testing, and into a working robot.

- 🔭 **Currently:** R&D Engineer at **JM Robotics AS**, Kristiansund, Norway.
- ⚙️ **My work:** Custom PCBs, embedded firmware, power electronics, hardware validation, and robot system integration.
- 🎓 **Education:** Erasmus Mundus **Marine and Maritime Intelligent Robotics (MIR)** programme at **NTNU** and the **University of Toulon**, following a BSc in **Robotics & Mechatronics Engineering** from the **University of Dhaka**.
- 🌊 **Research background:** Underwater vehicle dynamics, control systems, autonomous navigation, sensor fusion, and deep reinforcement learning.

## 🚀 What I'm exploring now

- **Physical AI and robot learning:** Building further skills in deep reinforcement learning, NVIDIA Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab, and sim-to-real transfer.
- **Underwater visual navigation:** Exploring AI-assisted monocular VSLAM with ROS 2 and GPU-based processing.
- **Complete robotic systems:** Bringing custom hardware, perception, control, and learned behaviour together.

## 🔬 Selected work

- **Miniature AUV:** Dynamic modelling, vertical-motion control, simulation, and prototype testing for my MSc thesis at NTNU.
- **Underwater navigation with DRL:** Unity-based simulation environments and BlueROV models for training and evaluating navigation policies.
- **Autonomous AUV docking:** A prototype combining docking, wireless data transmission, and wireless charging.
- **Autonomous ground robot:** Multimodal sensing and reinforcement learning for mapless navigation.
- **MoreFish:** Sensor amplifier PCBs and power management for a solar-powered aquaculture monitoring device.
